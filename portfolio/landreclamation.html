Land Reclamation

(publication, 2025)

Land Reclamation compiles the words and images from David J. Clarke's Reclaimed Land: Hong Kong in Transition and James Crawford's The Strange Saga of Kowloon Walled City. Together, these works document the physical and social transformation of Hong Kong during the 1997 handover from British to Chinese rule.
