ALLIE HEFFNER

is a graphic designer currently based in Brooklyn, New York. She is interested in exploring and understanding the relationship between craft and design - specifically, but not limited to, textiles and Graphic Design. Her works ranges from publications, brand identities, art direction, and editorial work.

You will always find her discovering and collecting new music, wearing patterns, and having fun!

She is currently at Portrait and Suko Magazine. Previously at Brown Student Radio, Weird Friends Studio, and A Work of Substance.

Mining Hall of Fame

Brand Identity, 2025

The Mining Hall of Fame rebrand aims to modernize perceptions of mining while preserving its rich history. Based in Leadville, Colorado, the rebrand pays homage to the mining boom that shaped the American West while honoring the workers and families whose lives were impacted by it.

Drawing inspiration from light and dark contrast, this identity uses silhouettes of miners, tools, rocks, and landscapes as the secondary visual elements of the brand.
