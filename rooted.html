She is interested in exploring the relationship between design and craft, experimenting with new materials while working with the old.

You will always find her discovering and collecting new music, browsing the Internet Archive, and crocheting one of her many unfinished projects.

Rooted

(brand identity, 2025)

Rooted brings together a space and a program built around a single act: getting to know and caring for people across generations. Together, through events, family programs, and buddy programs, it documents and nurtures the bonds that form between people of all ages.
