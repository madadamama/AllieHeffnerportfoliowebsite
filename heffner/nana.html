Nana's Kimono

2026.001.01

There are very few memories and moments that I remember with Nana. But the one I remember most clearly was when we visited her in Mount Kisco, sat in her living room, eating frozen blueberries.

I guess at 6 years old, the only things I associated with being frozen was ice cream. So when she brought out a frozen green lunchbox with little round things rolling around inside, I knew it was about to become my next obsession.

I love frozen blueberries and I sometimes miss my Nana.
