Tie Four Ways

2026.005.04

The most unconventional storyteller was my Dad. He would tell us about his time in high school, college, and the in-between. He never made it seem like a chore to sit down and tell my sister and me bedtime stories — maybe because he could reminisce while telling them.

One of the many was about when he caught his high school girlfriend cheating on him. They had made plans to go to a party together, but when he arrived he found her in the bathroom with one of the student athletes. The details of what they were doing were redacted — this was being told to a 10-year-old and a 15-year-old.

But he was good at telling stories. You felt like you were there in the room, taking in every piece of his life.
