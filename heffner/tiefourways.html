Tie Four Ways

2026.003.04

I grew up not knowing much about my Grandfather. I knew that he was a pastor, had no intentions of getting married, and ended up with four children. Everything that I knew about him was through word of mouth.

Through old newspapers and family stories, I began to piece together what his life may have looked like: what he liked to eat, what made him upset, what made him want to become a pastor, and how deeply his work shaped our family.

This fabric references his clerical collar and the visual language around it. It becomes a way to remember him through pattern, repetition, and print.
