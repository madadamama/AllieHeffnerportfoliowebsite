Mama Watch!

2026.007.01

My first trip to Seattle was roughly a year after my Dad had passed. My sister and I were welcomed with open arms to celebrate the holidays with Auntie Maria and her family — a somewhat full reunion, with the majority of the Heffners there.

We played mahjong, looked through old family photos, celebrated Christmas together, and talked candidly about how hard it was to grieve.
