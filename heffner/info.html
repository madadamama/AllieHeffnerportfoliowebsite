Backstory

I became an archivist when my Dad passed away. I had begun to collect and dig through mounds of files and photos when there were no more physical things I could hold onto.

I have always been drawn to family photos. Every time I would visit my family in Hong Kong, I would make sure to dedicate at least two days within my two-week stay to scanning and digitizing them. This resulted in getting in trouble with my Mom because she had a certain way of organizing them and I had messed up her "system."


The reward in messing up her "system" was a collection of messages from my family saying "Treasures! Thank you, Allie. Those were good times." or "Allie always manages to find our old photos." And this is all true. I continue to find joy in searching for memory. There are people I have no memories with in these pictures, some I don't even know, and some I wish I had more time with.
