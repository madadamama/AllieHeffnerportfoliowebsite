ALLIE HEFFNER

is a graphic designer currently based in Brooklyn, New York. She is interested in exploring and understanding the relationship between craft and design - specifically, but not limited to, textiles and Graphic Design. Her works ranges from publications, brand identities, art direction, and editorial work.

You will always find her discovering and collecting new music, wearing patterns, and having fun!

She is currently at Portrait and Suko Magazine. Previously at Brown Student Radio, Weird Friends Studio, and A Work of Substance.

An Interview with Sharlene Deng

Editorial, 2025

Alongside Neat Rodanant, we interviewed Sharlene Deng, a multidisciplinary graphic designer based in NYC, about her practice, her time at RISD, and her journey as a graphic designer.

Drawing inspiration from New York magazine spreads, each conversation from the interview was designed to feel like a separate story. Given the range of her multidisciplinary practice, it felt important to reflect that through difference in design and color; showcasing the distinct voices and skills that define her work.
