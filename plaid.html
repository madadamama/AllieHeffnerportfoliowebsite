PLAID

Typeface + Web + Publication, 2024

PLAID is an identity built from a lifelong love of the pattern. Accompanied by an interactive book and website, the project features a custom typeface alongside 26 original plaid patterns.

The custom typeface draws inspiration from weaving patterns and the variety of lines used in creating tartan.
