Brown Student Radio

(brand identity, ongoing)

Brown Student Radio (BSR) is a college radio station programmed and run by Brown University students and Rhode Island community members. At its core, BSR is a platform for cross-genre music shows, podcasts, and talk shows.

As Head of Design, I co-led a team of 8 designers in redesigning BSR's visual identity; creating a look that reflects what the station represents: a space and community for vibrant people with vibrant music tastes. Beyond the rebrand, I directed Valentine's Day merchandise production, designed promotional posters, and oversaw the visual output of the design team.

Learn more about what's going on @BSR!
